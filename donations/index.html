Help Build the Next 75 Years of Fishkill Little League

Help us raise $250,000 for equipment, fields and batting cages as we approach our 75th anniversary in 2029.

How to give

Give the next generation a place to play

Fishkill Little League started in 1954. By the late 1970s, volunteers were holding dances to raise money for a sports complex. Families helped build up the diamonds at Shepheard Memorial Park, and in 2024 the league played its first game under the lights. Each generation made the fields a little better for the kids who came next.

Today, volunteers still coach teams, prepare fields and run the league. As we approach our 75th anniversary, we’re inviting individuals, families, businesses and community organizations to help give the next generation a place to learn the game, make friends and belong.

What the campaign will support

Equipment

Replace worn shared gear and equip baseball and softball teams for practices and games.

Field improvements

Improve the playing surfaces and address wear in the areas that see the most use.

Batting cages

Upgrade our batting cages so coaches have better space for hitting instruction and players can get more practice.

Give equipment, materials or your time

We welcome in-kind donations from individuals, businesses and community organizations. If you have something to offer, here are a few ways it could help:

  • Equipment: Baseball and softball gear, practice nets or field maintenance tools.
  • Materials: Infield mix, lumber, fencing or batting cage netting.
  • Labor and services: Field work, equipment repair, fabrication, installation or hauling.
