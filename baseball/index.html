Baseball in Southern Dutchess County

Fishkill Little League offers coed baseball for players from Fishkill, Beacon, East Fishkill, Glenham, Brinckerhoff and nearby communities. First-time players can start with FUN-damentals, Tee Ball or Coach Pitch. Older players can compare player-pitch divisions below.

Our primary home is Robert G. Shepheard Memorial Park in Fishkill, NY. Email the league if you have questions about your player’s division or eligibility.

See school and residency eligibility or check an address in the Little League Finder.

Baseball Divisions

Baseball league age is based on your child’s age on August 31, 2027. Tee Ball has its own eligibility dates. Check the Tee Ball options in Sports Connect for birthday eligibility. FUN-damentals players must be 4 by the start of the season.

FUN-damentals

Age 4

A parent-and-player introduction to throwing, catching, hitting and running the bases.

View registration details

Tee Ball

Ages 5–6

Learn the basics by hitting from a tee. Choose Monday or Tuesday practice when you register.

View registration details

Coach Pitch

Ages 6–8

Hit pitches from a coach while learning field positions and game basics.

View registration details

Instructional Kid Pitch

Ages 7–9

Make the transition to player pitching with an emphasis on instruction and skill development.

View registration details

Minor Player Pitch

Ages 9–11

Build pitching, hitting and defensive skills in player-pitch games.

View registration details

Major

Ages 10–12

Develop game strategy and teamwork in the Little League Major division.

View registration details

Intermediate 50/70

Ages 11–13

Transition to a larger diamond with a 50-foot pitching distance and 70-foot basepaths.

View registration details

Junior

Ages 13–14

Play full-diamond baseball with 90-foot basepaths.

View registration details

Senior

Ages 14–16

Continue competitive full-diamond baseball for older players.

View registration details

Where Baseball Begins

Many Fishkill players begin in Tee Ball and continue through the Major, Intermediate, Junior and Senior levels.
