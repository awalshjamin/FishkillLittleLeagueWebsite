About Fishkill Little League

Serving Southern Dutchess Since 1954

Our Mission

Fishkill Little League provides baseball and softball opportunities for youth throughout Fishkill and surrounding communities. We strive to teach sportsmanship, teamwork, leadership, confidence, and respect while fostering a lifelong love of the game.

Serving Southern Dutchess

See baseball and softball eligibility by community.

Fishkill Little League welcomes families from Fishkill, Beacon, East Fishkill, Glenham, Brinckerhoff and surrounding communities. Founded in 1954, the league continues a tradition of player development, volunteerism, and community involvement spanning generations.

Use the Little League Finder to check whether your home or school address is within the league’s boundaries.

Students at all Beacon City School District schools are eligible, along with students at Fishkill Elementary, Gayhead Elementary, Brinckerhoff Elementary, St. Mary’s School, and John Jay High School.

Players league age 4–7 can register regardless of where they live or attend school. Older players may also qualify through uninterrupted participation or a sibling provision. Check player eligibility before registering.
